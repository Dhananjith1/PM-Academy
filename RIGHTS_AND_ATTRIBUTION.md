## Sri Lankan copyright context

The Intellectual Property Act, No. 36 of 2003 protects original works, including writings and computer programs. Section 8 excludes ideas, procedures, systems, methods of operation, concepts, principles, discoveries, and mere data from copyright protection. Section 9 gives rights holders specified economic rights, including reproduction and communication to the public. Section 11 sets out the general fair-use framework and four factors: the purpose and character of the use (including commercial or non-profit educational purpose), the nature of the work, the amount and substantiality used, and the effect on the work's potential market or value. Section 12 identifies specific acts treated as fair use, including short quotations and certain teaching uses, subject to their own conditions. These provisions are fact-specific; a public website should not assume that educational purpose alone makes copying lawful.

An educational purpose is not blanket permission to reproduce an entire guide, course, diagram, or substantial extract on a public website. Short quotations and teaching illustrations still need to satisfy the Act's conditions, source/author attribution rules, and any applicable source licence. This section is general information, not legal advice.

Official source: [National Intellectual Property Office of Sri Lanka — Intellectual Property Act No. 36 of 2003](https://www.nipo.gov.lk/web/images/pdf_downloads/Intellectual_Property_Act_No_36_of_2003.pdf).
