# Independent Legal Review Brief

**Purpose:** Organise a qualified review of the public project-management learning website and its repository.  
**Status:** Preparation brief only — not legal advice, a legal opinion, or clearance.

## 1. Project at a glance

- Public-facing title: **Project Management Study Resource**
- Historical GitHub repository: https://github.com/Dhananjith1/PM-Academy
- Current Pages URL: https://dhananjith1.github.io/PM-Academy/
- Content: 27 introductory PM lessons, an Earned Value Management calculator, a glossary, and a 13-question learning quiz.
- Hosting: Static GitHub Pages.
- Data handling: lesson-completion IDs and visitor-counter deduplication/cache flags in browser localStorage; a custom counter script on every public page contacts the external Abacus endpoint at abacus.jasoncameron.dev. Provider-side logging, retention, processing location, and privacy terms have not been independently verified.
- Development: AI-assisted front-end implementation and educational wording; human authorship, editing, ownership, and licensing of individual parts should be reviewed rather than assumed.

## 2. Matters for written advice

### A. Name, trademark and prior use

The former public-facing wording was “PM Academy”. A preliminary public-web search identified same-name businesses in the project-management training sector:
- https://pmacademy.co.uk/
- https://pmacademy.africa/about

The public-facing title has been changed to a descriptive phrase. The old GitHub repository slug and Pages path remain for link continuity. Please advise on:
- Sri Lankan exact/similar/phonetic/conceptual trademark searches;
- prior registered and unregistered use, passing-off and confusion risk;
- the appropriate Nice classes for the activities actually offered;
- the effect of Section 144 of the Intellectual Property Act, No. 36 of 2003 on prior unregistered trade-name use and whether the historical repository/Pages slug should be changed, redirected, or otherwise handled;
- any separate search required before use outside Sri Lanka.

Section 144 of the Sri Lankan Intellectual Property Act No. 36 of 2003 addresses protection of trade names even before or without registration and potentially misleading later use of the same or a similar trade name. The exact application to the project and to prior overseas businesses requires legal advice. The official Sri Lankan NIPO database is https://nipo.lk.wipo.net/. WIPO's Global Brand Database FAQ cautions that no result is proof of availability and recommends national-office searching: https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/faqs_branddb.

### B. Third-party marks, linking and historical use

The current public learning page uses PMI®, PMP®, CAPM®, PMI-ACP®, the *PMBOK® Guide*, Jira, and Trello only to identify the relevant publisher, credentials, and software products. No third-party logo, certification seal, or branded framework artwork is intentionally used.

Please review:
- whether the current descriptive use and attribution of PMI word marks is appropriate;
- the implications of PMI's Digital Properties Linking Agreement, which restricts link placement to approved webpages and excludes home/about-me sections; the project has removed direct PMI hyperlinks because no separate approval has been obtained;
- Atlassian's published trademark conditions for descriptive product references;
- third-party certification/framework marks and branded lesson material that appeared in earlier Git history but have been removed from the current public learning page;
- whether any historical public use requires further correction, notice, or other action.

The current default branch does not include direct links to PMI properties or the removed third-party branded framework/certification material.

### C. Copyright, AI assistance and source material

Please review:
- the extent to which lesson wording, examples, quiz questions, design, code, and graphics are original and owned by the project creator;
- provenance and applicable terms for AI-assisted output;
- whether any wording, figures, charts, diagrams, examples, or screenshots may reproduce protected third-party expression;
- the CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution and ShareAlike scope for the identified Scrum Guide and Open Guide to Kanban summary portions;
- the current Rights and Reuse Notice and the historical MIT licence present in earlier repository commits, including any permissions already granted to people who accessed earlier versions.

Official reference: Sri Lanka Intellectual Property Act No. 36 of 2003 — https://www.nipo.gov.lk/web/images/pdf_downloads/Intellectual_Property_Act_No_36_of_2003.pdf.

### D. Privacy, accessibility, consumer-facing disclaimers

Please review the public Privacy Notice and Rights & Educational Disclaimer; the GitHub Pages and Abacus counter data flows; browser localStorage keys; provider terms, location, security, retention and deletion; the absence of a private privacy-request contact channel; and the educational/non-certification statements. Assess whether a data-processing agreement, impact assessment, transfer assessment, privacy contact, further disclosure, or counter-provider replacement/removal is required before the relevant commencement of Sri Lanka's PDPA. As at 11 November 2026, the DPA's published notice says Gazette 2498/16 sets 1 January 2027 for commencement of section 2, section 3, Part I and Part III; Part II and Part VII are stated as not yet in force. Confirm the current position and territorial scope directly before relying on it. Also assess any consumer-protection obligations if the site later offers paid products or services.
