# Preliminary Trademark and Naming Risk Screen

**Review date:** 11 November 2026  
**Public-facing title after mitigation:** Project Management Study Resource  
**Historical repository/Pages slug:** PM-Academy

## Executive finding

The former public-facing name **PM Academy** presents an avoidable naming risk because public-web search identifies other businesses using the same wording for project-management training and closely related professional-development services. This does not, by itself, prove infringement or determine who owns enforceable rights in Sri Lanka. It is enough to make continued use as the title/brand imprudent until a proper clearance search is completed.

The public title and header have therefore been changed to the descriptive phrase **Project Management Study Resource**. No claim is made that this phrase has been registered, cleared, or exclusively owned. The legacy repository and Pages path remain unchanged only to preserve existing links.

## Public-web findings

- [PM Academy UK](https://pmacademy.co.uk/) publicly uses “PM Academy” for project-management, Agile, change-management, and related professional training. Its website identifies PM Academy as a trading name of Ennerdale Bridge Limited.
- [PM Academy Africa](https://pmacademy.africa/about) publicly uses “PM Academy” for project-management training, consulting, and recruitment services, and describes an operation established in 2005.

These are primary business websites and establish actual public use of the wording in a closely related field. They do not establish the legal status or priority of marks in Sri Lanka or every other country. No conclusion is made about infringement.

## What this review did and did not do

This was a limited public-web risk screen on 11 November 2026. It was not:
- a formal NIPO register search or certified search report;
- a WIPO Global Brand Database search report;
- an examination of every national, regional, company-name, domain-name, common-law, or unregistered right;
- a legal opinion on infringement, passing off, ownership, or registrability;
- a guarantee that the replacement descriptive title or legacy URL is risk-free.

WIPO's official FAQ says automatic/robotic searches are prohibited by its database terms, explains that national data may be updated with delays, and says that no matching result does not mean a name is available. It recommends checking the relevant national IP office as well. See [WIPO Global Brand Database FAQs](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/faqs_branddb) and [WIPO's official availability guidance](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/madrid-system/check-availability).

The Sri Lankan official portal is the [NIPO IP Database](https://nipo.lk.wipo.net/). Its records must be searched manually by a competent person; this project has not obtained an official NIPO clearance result.

## Legal framework to discuss with counsel

Sri Lanka's [Intellectual Property Act, No. 36 of 2003](https://www.parliament.lk/uploads/acts/gbills/english/3726.pdf) includes provisions on marks capable of distinguishing goods/services and on conflicting earlier marks and trade names. Its copyright provisions also protect qualifying expression while excluding ideas, procedures, systems, methods, concepts, and principles. A lawyer should review the current Act together with applicable regulations and facts.

Scaled Agile states that SAFe® and Scaled Agile Framework® are its registered marks and restricts copying or modifying its courseware and copyrighted content; this site uses the name only as a reference and links to the official source. See [Scaled Agile's usage restrictions](https://scaledagile.com/digital-badging-usage-restrictions/) and [content-use FAQ](https://support.scaledagile.com/en/articles/9791354-general-content-usage-faqs-and-the-permission-request-form). PMI publishes [Trademark Usage Guidelines](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/governance/intellectual-property-proper-use-guidelines.pdf), PeopleCert publishes a [Marks Usage Policy](https://www.peoplecert.org/-/media/folders-reorganized/legal-documents/qmepo13-marks-usage-policy.pdf), and Atlassian publishes [Trademark Guidelines](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/trademark). These are owners' policies and may change; this site links to them as references, not as a substitute for legal review.

## Required professional clearance steps

A qualified Sri Lankan IP/trademark lawyer or registered IP agent should:
1. Search NIPO for exact, similar, phonetic, visual, and conceptual variants of the former name and replacement title.
2. Review relevant Nice classes and actual goods/services. Likely areas to assess include education/training and any software, downloadable content, publishing, or consultancy services actually offered; counsel must decide the correct classes.
3. Check prior registered and unregistered use, company/business names, domains, social accounts, and relevant foreign markets.
4. Advise whether the old repository slug and historical public use need further action.
5. Review third-party mark references, the historical MIT licence in earlier commits, copyright provenance, and any AI-assisted material.
6. Provide written advice on whether the public title, repository path, and planned future use can continue.

For a minor project owner, a parent/guardian or responsible adult should coordinate any formal legal engagement, costs, and filings.

## Current mitigation

- The public site title and top navigation display use the descriptive phrase “PM Study Resource” / “Independent Project Management Study Resource”.
- The site does not claim to be affiliated with, approved by, or accredited by PMI, PeopleCert, Scrum.org, Scaled Agile, Atlassian, or other referenced providers.
- Third-party word marks are used only to identify the relevant publications, certifications, or products; no third-party logos or certification seals are intentionally included in the reviewed HTML.
- The current page's social-preview image metadata has been removed rather than relying on an unverified older graphic.
- The repository slug and published URL remain historical technical identifiers, not a trademark-availability statement.

**Status:** Preliminary risk mitigation only. Trademark clearance remains outstanding.
