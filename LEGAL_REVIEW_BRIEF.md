# Independent Legal Review Brief

**Purpose:** Organise a qualified review of the public project-management learning website and its repository.  
**Status:** Preparation brief only — not legal advice, a legal opinion, or clearance.

## 1. Project at a glance

- Public-facing title: **Project Management Study Resource**
- Historical GitHub repository: https://github.com/Dhananjith1/PM-Academy
- Current Pages URL: https://dhananjith1.github.io/PM-Academy/
- Content: 27 introductory PM lessons, an Earned Value Management calculator, a glossary, and a 13-question learning quiz.
- Hosting: Static GitHub Pages.
- Data handling: Browser localStorage for lesson-completion IDs; no site accounts, contact forms, payment flow, or analytics are included in the reviewed source.
- Development: AI-assisted front-end implementation and educational wording; human authorship, editing, ownership, and licensing of individual parts should be reviewed rather than assumed.

## 2. Matters for written advice

### A. Name, trademark and prior use
The former public-facing wording was “PM Academy”. A preliminary public-web search identified same-name businesses in the project-management training sector:
- https://pmacademy.co.uk/
- https://pmacademy.africa/about

The public-facing title has been changed to a descriptive phrase. The old GitHub repository slug and Pages path remain for link continuity. Please advise on:
- Sri Lankan exact/similar/phonetic/conceptual trademark searches;
- prior registered and unregistered use, passing-off and confusion risk;
- the appropriate Nice classes for the activities actually offered;
- the effect of Section 144 of the Intellectual Property Act, No. 36 of 2003 on prior unregistered trade-name use and whether the historical repository/Pages slug should be changed, redirected, or otherwise handled;
- any separate search required before use outside Sri Lanka.

Section 144 of the Sri Lankan Intellectual Property Act No. 36 of 2003 addresses protection of trade names even before or without registration and potentially misleading later use of the same or a similar trade name. The exact application to the project and to prior overseas businesses requires legal advice. The official Sri Lankan NIPO database is https://nipo.lk.wipo.net/. WIPO's Global Brand Database FAQ cautions that no result is not proof of availability and recommends national-office searching: https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/faqs_branddb.

### B. Third-party marks and references
The site refers to PMI®, PMP®, CAPM®, PMI-ACP®, the *PMBOK® Guide*, PRINCE2®, SAFe®, Scaled Agile Framework®, Jira, Trello, Scrum, and their relevant owners/publishers. It includes ownership/independence statements and official links. Please review the placement, exact mark status, symbols, nominative use, disclaimers, and whether any owner-specific permission or wording is needed.

Policies provided for review:
- PMI: https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/governance/intellectual-property-proper-use-guidelines.pdf
- PeopleCert: https://www.peoplecert.org/-/media/folders-reorganized/legal-documents/qmepo13-marks-usage-policy.pdf
- Atlassian: https://www.atlassian.com/legal/trademark
- Scaled Agile: https://scaledagile.com/digital-badging-usage-restrictions/
- Scaled Agile Content Usage FAQ: https://support.scaledagile.com/en/articles/9791354-general-content-usage-faqs-and-the-permission-request-form

Please also review SAFe® and Scaled Agile Framework® as Scaled Agile, Inc. registered trademarks and its content-use restrictions. The project has added a source link and owner attribution, but this does not establish permission for any proprietary content.

### C. Copyright, AI assistance and source material
Please review:
- the extent to which lesson wording, examples, quiz questions, design, code, and graphics are original and owned by the project creator;
- provenance and applicable terms for AI-assisted output;
- whether any wording, figures, charts, diagrams, examples, or screenshots may reproduce protected third-party expression;
- the Scrum Guide's CC BY-SA 4.0 terms and whether the site's summaries are independent enough to avoid creating an adaptation;
- the current Rights and Reuse Notice and the historical MIT licence present in earlier repository commits, including any permissions already granted to people who accessed earlier versions.

Official reference: Sri Lanka Intellectual Property Act No. 36 of 2003 — https://www.parliament.lk/uploads/acts/gbills/english/3726.pdf.

### D. Privacy, accessibility, consumer-facing disclaimers
Please check the Privacy Notice, external links, GitHub Pages hosting, localStorage explanation, educational/non-certification disclaimer, and any future monetisation or commercial offers. Confirm whether additional privacy or consumer-law disclosures are needed for intended users and jurisdictions.
