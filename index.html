About PM Academy

Mission & Purpose: PM Academy was created as a free-to-access educational resource and developer portfolio project to make project management concepts intuitive, accessible, and practical. Formal reference standards and certification preparation courses can often be lengthy or costly, and learners benefit from an approachable primer that breaks down scheduling, EVM formulas, and delivery workflows into clear, modular lessons.

Target Audience: Aspiring project coordinators, software engineers, startup founders, cross-functional team leaders, and students seeking a foundational conceptual primer before pursuing formal industry training.

PM Academy — Project Overview & Architecture

1. Problem & Purpose

The Problem: Formal reference standards and comprehensive certification preparation courses can often be lengthy or costly, and learners benefit from a practical, interactive primer connecting delivery governance with everyday tools.

The Purpose: Create an accessible, lightweight, zero-dependency educational application that teaches core project management mechanics, agile practices, EVM mathematics, and tool selection in clear, conversational English.

2. Target Audience

• Junior Project Managers, Associate PMs, and Project Coordinators seeking quick practical references.

• Software engineers, tech leads, and founders wanting structured delivery methodologies.

• Students preparing for formal certification studies who need a conceptual foundation.

3. Key Features

• 27 Modular Lessons in 6 Progressive Parts: Covering Foundations, Planning, Risk & Change, Agile Frameworks, PM Tool ecosystem (12+ reviews), and Leadership & Career paths.

• Live EVM Mathematical Calculator: Instant recalculation of CV, SV, CPI, SPI, and EAC with automated health interpretations.

• Interactive Scenario Quiz: 12 scenario-based conceptual questions with detailed pedagogical feedback cards.

• Instant Glossary Filter: Client-side instant keyword lookup across 35 fundamental PM concepts.

• Progress Persistence: Browser LocalStorage synchronization preserving completed lesson markers.

4. Independence & Third-Party Notice

• Independent Synthesis: Curriculum summaries, analogies, worked calculations, and assessment items are educational syntheses created to discuss standard project management topics.

• Plain-Text Reference: No official logos or certification seals are displayed; product and framework names (such as PMBOK®, PRINCE2®, Scrum, Jira, Trello) are used strictly in plain text for identification and comparative discussion. All third-party marks remain the property of their respective rights holders.

• Non-Affiliation: Independent project with no commercial affiliation, partnership, or accreditation from PMI, PeopleCert, or other credentialing bodies.

5. Architecture & Technical Design

• Tech Stack: Pure Vanilla HTML5, modern CSS3 (Custom Properties & Grid/Flexbox), and modular ES6 JavaScript.

• Performance: Zero build dependencies, lightweight single-bundle architecture, fast client-side rendering.

• Accessibility & Responsive Design: Designed with responsive layouts and accessibility considerations across mobile and desktop devices.
